Atu Farm: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Atu Farm ("the game") is a mobile game for Android, published by BlackByte Studios. This page explains what information the game uses. Contact: BlackKakapo@protonmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The game does not ask you to create an account. Your progress (levels, stars, coins, diamonds, the farm, the aquarium, your settings and statistics) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, the progress is lost, unless Android's own backup restores it for you.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game may show a reminder on your phone (for example when something on the farm is ready). These are created on the device; no information about you is sent anywhere to make them.

Ads

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the first screen and full-screen ads between runs. To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, the type of device and the ads you see or tap. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent to ads before asking for them, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the game, under Settings → Privacy and ads choices (shown where required). You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address, with a new date.
